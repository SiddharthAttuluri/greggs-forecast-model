# Source documents

The scripts expect Greggs plc's annual reports here, renamed as follows. They are not
committed to the repository; download them from the investors section of Greggs plc's
website.

| File | Document | Period |
|---|---|---|
| AR21.pdf | Annual Report and Accounts 2021 | 52 weeks to 1 January 2022 |
| AR22.pdf | Annual Report 2022 | 52 weeks to 31 December 2022 |
| AR23.pdf | Annual Report and Accounts 2023 | 52 weeks to 30 December 2023 |
| AR24.pdf | Annual Report and Accounts 2024 | 52 weeks to 28 December 2024 |
| AR25.pdf | Annual Report and Accounts 2025 | 52 weeks to 27 December 2025 |
| H1_2026.pdf | Interim results presentation, 29 July 2026 | 26 weeks to 27 June 2026 |

Page numbers in the data file are PDF page numbers (printed page number plus two).
